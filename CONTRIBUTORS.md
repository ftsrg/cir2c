## History

cir2c began inside [ftsrg/cir-demo](https://github.com/ftsrg/cir-demo), where it
was developed alongside a web demonstrator and a verification benchmark suite.
It was extracted into this repository with its commit history intact, so
`git log` and `git blame` still attribute the work correctly.

## Acknowledgements

cir2c is built on [ClangIR](https://llvm.github.io/clangir/) and
[MLIR](https://mlir.llvm.org/), and would not be possible without the upstream
LLVM contributors who are bringing ClangIR into `llvm-project`.
